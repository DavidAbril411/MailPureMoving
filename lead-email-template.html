📋 Policies & Guidelines

Weight Limits

Please be advised that the weight of your items must not exceed 8,000 lbs per truck due to DOT & FMCSA regulations. THE DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION WILL NOT ALLOW US TO MOVE YOUR ITEMS IF THEY WEIGH MORE THAN 8,000 LBS. Since it is virtually impossible for anyone to accurately guess the weight of your stuff in advance, we will use weight sensors on the day of the move to make sure we can safely get on the road. In the incredibly rare event that the items weigh more than 8,000 LBS, we guarantee that we will not take advantage of you by charging crazy storage rates or excessive fees of any kind. However, please be advised that you may incur our cost to deliver the rest of your items in another truck at a later date. We guarantee that you will only be asked to pay our cost in this scenario as we have no desire whatsoever to extract profits from unexpected & rare situations such as these.

Double Drive Time Explained

In the state of California, all licensed & insured moving companies double the time that the truck spends driving between your pick-up & drop-off locations. This is not a charge that we created, it is a regulation set in place by the state of California. So, for example, if it takes us 30 minutes to drive from your pick-up location to your drop-off location, you will be charged for 1 hour of driving on your final invoice and that's it. This is why we do not charge you for the time it will take our truck & movers to drive from our parking lot to your pick-up location OR for the time it will take them to drive back to our parking lot from your drop-off location.

We Have a 4 Hour Minimum Requirement

If your move is completed in less than 4 hours you will be charged for 4 hours. If your move takes more than 4 hours, you'll be charged for the actual time it takes to complete your move. You will be billed in 15-minute increments after the 4 hour minimum is reached.

Length of the Move

We are unable to determine the duration or final cost of any move in advance due to multiple variables that are unique to every move. Someone (either you or someone you have designated) should be present with our movers for the duration of your move. Please let us know in advance if you have an extenuating circumstance and will not be able to be there.

Payment

We accept ALL major credit/debit cards or cash, but we do not accept checks of ANY kind. You will receive a receipt at the end of your move regardless of your form of payment.

Parking

Customers are fully responsible for parking or truck violations during the move time. If you do not have a driveway for us to pull into, please secure legal street parking for your truck on move day. Please make yourself aware of any weight limit or truck restrictions on your street. You may need a permit for the truck. If you feel that your street may have truck restrictions it is best to: GOOGLE your ZIP CODE + MOVING TRUCK PERMIT

TVs & Computer Monitors

Televisions & computer monitors will be turned on & tested before and after the move for service quality assurance. Please keep the power cables UNPACKED until the movers have a chance to complete the test.

Appliances & Dressers

We require any appliance or piece of furniture we move be completely empty to avoid any internal damage or leakage. We do not offer a waiver for this policy because we have seen too many unexpected incidents that could have been avoided by completely emptying the piece. Even clothes inside of the drawers in a dresser can potentially lead to internal damage to your furniture. Please be advised that we are unable to reconnect any appliance's water or gas lines for multiple liability reasons.

Pianos, Safes, & Large/Heavy Items

If you have a PIANO, LARGE SAFE, LARGE MARBLE TABLE, or any other items that could be considered excessively heavy (300 lbs or more), please notify us immediately as we will need to send a specialized crew, truck with a lift gate, & item specific tools. We will not move items over 300 lbs without at least 48 hours notice.

What If I Need to Cancel My Move?

We totally understand that things pop up & plans change so we have a no-hassle cancellation policy. However, if you need to cancel or change your date you need to call us or reply to this email asap to let us know. We will do our absolute best to reschedule you for your date of choice based on availability. Please keep in mind that the date you request to switch to might be a different rate. Please give us a minimum of 48 hours if you need to change or cancel your move date.
